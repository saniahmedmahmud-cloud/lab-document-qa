FRIDGE TEMPERATURE
The reagent fridge temperature is checked twice a day, at 8 am and 4 pm.
The accepted range is 2 to 8 degrees Celsius.
If the temperature is outside the range, tell the lab supervisor at once.

QUALITY CONTROL
Quality control samples are run at the start of every shift.
Results are recorded in the QC logbook.
If a QC result is outside the accepted limits, the supervisor decides before patient results are reported.

SAMPLE REJECTION
A sample with no label is rejected and the ward is asked to send a new one.
A sample in the wrong tube is rejected.
The rejection is recorded in the rejection logbook.

LOGBOOK
The logbook is signed at the end of every shift.
Corrections are made with a single line through the mistake, with initials and date.
